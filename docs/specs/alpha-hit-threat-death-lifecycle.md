# Alpha hit contact, threat and direct fatal continuation

This contract covers original client RVAs 0x195A60, 0x1A0970 and 0x19B180, and the explicitly identified dependencies below. It describes native data effects and ordering, including effects that can precede rejection of health application. It does not specify a replacement implementation. Earlier held contracts were neither consulted nor changed during this scope.

The supported original image is `binary/Cube.exe`, SHA-256 `84A7A132A84D4282338E7EA45A1940D32066D64E39CBAFED8F2418D5A6DC30BF`, length 3,885,568 bytes, PE32 machine 0x014C, image base 0x00400000, image size 0x003BC000 and timestamp 0x51EA955E. Every evidence path below is relative to the original Alpha evidence root. Function filenames contain virtual addresses; the accompanying addresses in this prose are RVAs. Original assembly takes precedence where the decompiler omits reachable instructions or loses temporary-container state.

## Objects, identifiers and admission

The applier receives a world, a hit record, an outgoing collection, and an additional context passed through to death loot. The hit's source key occupies record +0x00 through +0x07; its target key occupies +0x08 through +0x0F; the signed float amount is +0x10; the category byte is +0x45. World +0x04 is the creature map, +0xB8 the local controlled-creature pointer and +0xB4 the mode byte. A creature's own eight-byte identifier is +0x08. An eight-byte key supplied by the hit, a creature-map entry key and an object's own identifier are distinct storage locations: the native routines use the particular location identified below rather than repairing disagreements between them. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x195AD2–0x195B3E and 0x1A098F–0x1A09B4.]

The target key must resolve to a nonnull creature whose float health at +0x16C is strictly positive. Otherwise this invocation has no contact, threat or fatal continuation. With no local pointer and target numeric role zero, the hit is instead appended through RVA 0x086290 and this applier returns. The later handling of that forwarded record is outside this scope. Other admitted targets undergo a separate source-key lookup; an absent or null source does not by itself reject the invocation. No special rejection of a zero identifier or of source/target aliasing occurs in this lookup. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x195AD2–0x195B3E.]

Throughout this document, **numeric role** means the byte at creature +0x60. It is separate from the character-class byte +0x140 and specialization byte +0x141. Species is the signed integer +0x64, level is the signed integer +0x190, and rank is the unsigned byte +0x1A8. Creature +0x11D0 is an eight-byte contact identifier. The map at +0x13AC holds float contact counts keyed by identifiers; the separate map at +0x13A4 holds float threat values. “Contact” and “threat” here describe these observed uses, without assigning a complete gameplay meaning to either map. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x195B57–0x195CDF, 0x196CAB–0x196D19 and 0x1966B1–0x1967FD.]

## Bookkeeping before the protected-target gate

A positive amount first reduces target float +0x1190 by the amount multiplied by 0.25, divided by a power with base 2 and exponent 0.25 × (target level − 1 + target rank); a negative result is replaced by zero. The exponent's integer sum becomes a float before multiplication by 0.25, and the power result becomes a float before the division. Target ability byte +0x68 equal to 84 is changed to zero and target timer +0x12C becomes 3000. This document gives +0x1190 no additional semantic name. These writes precede contact propagation and the flag-0x2000 admission test. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x195B3E–0x195BD5; original constants in `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs 0x345D98 and 0x345E18.]

For a nonnegative amount with a resolved source, the source's contact identifier becomes the hit's target key and the target's contact identifier becomes the hit's source key. The source contact-count entry keyed by the hit's target key increases by 1. The target threat entry keyed by the hit's source key receives the amount contribution, followed by addition of float 0.01, followed by addition of its former stored float. Missing entries are inserted with zero before arithmetic. Thus even a zero-amount hit can create contact and positive threat. The applier then calls RVA 0x1A0970 with the resolved source, resolved target and outgoing collection. These operations contain no random draw. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x195BD5–0x195CE4; `decompiled/client/functions/00468ad0.c`, RVA 0x068AD0.]

The amount multiplier is 1 when the source has character class 3, species 117, 86 or 104, or an item at +0xAA8 of category 3 and subtype 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 or 12. Otherwise species 25 selects multiplier 10. Otherwise multiplier 20 requires category 3 at +0xAA8, subtype 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 15, 16, 17 or 18, and specialization 1. The remaining case uses multiplier 2. This precedence matters where predicates overlap. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00444520.c`, RVA 0x044520; `decompiled/client/functions/00444820.c`, RVA 0x044820; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x195C2F–0x195CC1.]

## Propagation at RVA 0x1A0970

The propagation routine first obtains the target threat entry keyed by the **source object's own identifier**, inserting zero if missing. It appends an outgoing +0x50 record containing target object's own identifier, source object's own identifier and the current stored float. It does not add another amount at this point. This first record is emitted regardless of the stored value's sign. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1A098F–0x1A0A0A; `decompiled/client/functions/00468ad0.c`, RVA 0x068AD0.]

It then traverses the world creature map in its native key order. A candidate must be nonnull, differ by pointer from both supplied objects, pass the relationship predicate with **candidate first and source second**, and lie strictly within eight world units of the target. Distance uses each signed 64-bit fixed-position difference at +0x10, +0x18 and +0x20, rounds each difference to float, multiplies by float 1/65536, squares in float, adds Y squared to X squared and then adds Z squared. The result must be strictly below float 64. There is no candidate-health test. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1A0A20–0x1A0B11; `binary/Cube.exe`, constants at RVAs 0x2FCD94 and 0x2FD330.]

For each qualifying candidate, the candidate threat entry keyed by the source object's identifier is obtained with zero insertion. An entry exactly equal to zero, including negative zero, becomes float 0.5 and produces another outgoing +0x50 record containing candidate identifier, source identifier and 0.5. Any nonzero value is preserved; this is neither an additive 0.5 nor a minimum clamp. An unordered float comparison does not select the zero case. New records follow candidate traversal order. The routine has no random draw and does not recursively propagate from newly enrolled candidates. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1A0B13–0x1A0BCA.]

The relationship predicate at RVA 0x196CA0 rejects numeric-role pair 5 then 0, rejects a first role of 6, and rejects a second role of 6. Subject to those exclusions, exactly one role of 1 makes the result true. With both roles 1, unequal results from RVA 0x044680 make it true. Cases not already accepted are true when bit 0x20 in either creature's byte +0x124 is set; otherwise they are false. In particular, equal results for two role-1 creatures still reach the +0x124 flag tests. The role-5/role-0 exclusion is directional. No source/target ownership identifier is consulted by this predicate. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x196CAB–0x196D2A.]

RVA 0x044680 is false when any bit of word +0x7E mask 0x1A00 is set. Otherwise it is true precisely for species in this hexadecimal set: 13, 14, 16, 17, 19, 1A, 1B, 1E, 1F, 20, 21, 22, 23, 35, 37, 38, 39, 3A, 43, 44, 4A, 57, 59, 5C, 5D, 62, 64, 6A, 6B, 91, 92, 93. These are numeric classifications, not established faction names. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x044680–0x044759.]

A negative amount has a different, later threat path: after health restoration and its upper bound, mode zero traverses nonnull world creatures, uses the relationship predicate with **resolved source first and candidate second**, and requires that candidate's threat map already contain the target object's own identifier. Its source-identifier entry then receives subtraction of the negative amount multiplied by float 0.1, followed by propagation with source and candidate. There is no positive-value requirement on the preexisting target entry. The source is not checked for null at this nested relationship call; the behavior of a missing-source negative record reaching this path is not established as a valid supported case. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60, negative-amount branch; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, corresponding calls to RVAs 0x196CA0, 0x068AD0 and 0x1A0970.]

## Flag 0x2000 and reaching a fatal continuation

After the initial nonnegative contact updates and propagation, a target with word +0x7E bit 0x2000 requires at least one threat entry whose float is strictly positive and whose key resolves to a nonnull numeric-role-0 creature. The first qualifying entry admits further processing. The qualifying creature need not be the hit source, alive, nearby or the local pointer. A newly updated entry can admit the same invocation. An absent qualifying entry returns after the earlier +0x1190, ability, timer, contact and threat effects. It prevents subsequent category handling, motion, health and the direct fatal continuation. There is no random draw in this test. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x195CE7–0x195D70.]

Admission does not itself guarantee health subtraction. Category 3 ends further application; categories 1 and 4 have separate non-health actions. The ordinary health section requires mode zero or the target to equal the local pointer. For an ordinary positive amount outside category 5, both target and any present source must pass RVA 0x03E310 before subtraction. That predicate accepts numeric roles 0, 1, 3 and 5, and role 6 except species 140, 141 and 142; it rejects other roles. A missing source bypasses the source predicate. Category 5 instead operates on type-6 resident buff records. Negative amounts restore health with an upper-bound query. This scope does not replace the separate semantics of those categories with a generic damage action. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60; `decompiled/client/functions/0043e310.c`, RVA 0x03E310.]

The fatal continuation examined here begins only at the applier's subsequent test that target +0x16C is at most zero. The target was required to be alive on entry. Merely writing zero health from some other routine does not call this continuation through an already-dead invocation of this applier. Nor does the linked-creature zero-health write below recursively invoke it. No conclusion is made about separate death-detection paths elsewhere in the client. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60.]

## Fatal world effects before relationship cleanup

The continuation stores health zero, appends sound identifier 27 at the target's fixed position with both remaining sound scalars 1, and clears target integer +0x6C. It looks up the zone using target cached signed integers +0x1B0 and +0x1B4. In that zone's ordered structure array, it selects the first record with kind 5, record integer +0x178 equal to target +0x1B8, and nonzero state byte +0x30. That record receives a state-zero change through RVA 0x1A58E0; a building-update tuple of cached zone coordinates, structure index and copied record is appended to outgoing +0x30. The search stops at that record. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60; `decompiled/client/functions/005a58e0.c`, RVA 0x1A58E0; `decompiled/client/functions/00468670.c`, RVA 0x068670; `decompiled/client/functions/004dd700.c`, RVA 0x0DD700.]

For this state transition, RVA 0x1A58E0 replaces record +0x34 with the greater of zero and 1000 minus its former value, stores state zero and appends sound 51 at the record's fixed position with sound scalars 1. The selected record's old nonzero state makes this transition active. There is no random draw in this helper. The subsequent call is death loot at RVA 0x19B180, using the dead target and the applier's additional context; its complete bounded ordering is given below. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/005a58e0.c`, RVA 0x1A58E0; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, fatal call site in RVA 0x195A60.]

After loot, an intervening traversal computes position differences for nonnull role-0 creatures through RVA 0x02C7A0 into a temporary vector; the inspected helper writes that temporary and exposes no persistent effect. Next, a nonzero identifier at dead target +0x11C8 is looked up. A found nonnull linked creature has its health set directly to zero. The dead target's link identifier remains stored. No ownership, species, numeric-role or previous-health condition is imposed on this linked object here. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60; `decompiled/client/functions/0042c7a0.c`, RVA 0x02C7A0.]

For reward arithmetic, C(x) denotes the native float curve 1 − 1 / (1 + 0.05 × (x − 1)). The dead target's level plus unsigned rank is converted to float and passed to this curve. Its result multiplied by 20 is bounded below by 1. Species 101, 107, 108, 109, 114, 115, 116, 117, 118 or 119 multiplies that result by 20. Flag 0x200 multiplies it by 10; flag 0x800 finally replaces it with zero. A later kill record truncates the resulting float toward zero to a signed integer. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/0043ca60.c`, RVA 0x03CA60; `decompiled/client/functions/004445d0.c`, RVA 0x0445D0; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, fatal reward arithmetic in RVA 0x195A60; constants in `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs 0x2FCD74, 0x2FCDB4, 0x345DC0 and 0x345E74.]

Mode zero can also update a region record located from cached zone coordinates divided by 8 with signed truncation toward zero. The region must exist, its state byte +0x41 must differ from 2, and the dead target must have flag 0x2000 or have nonzero byte +0x1D38 while region kind +0x34 is 5. The dead target's threat map is then searched for a strictly positive entry resolving to a nonnull role-0 creature. At the first such entry, region kinds 1–5 and 13 become state 2 and mark completion. Kinds 7–12 increment integer +0x44 and become state 1; reaching or exceeding +0x48 instead makes state 2 and marks completion. Other kinds produce no region update. A recognized kind appends the changed region record, with region coordinates, to outgoing +0x60. This eligibility uses the dead target's threat before the later global removals. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60; `decompiled/client/functions/00468620.c`, RVA 0x068620; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1963F1–0x196685.]

Completion visits the aligned 8-by-8 zone block, X outermost and Y innermost, using signed-truncated coordinate division. Each existing zone's pointer vector at +0x18/+0x1C has every nonnull pointer from the zone's saved count +0xA0 through its current end passed to its first virtual function with argument 1. The vector is then resized to the saved count. These calls can destroy pointed objects; their concrete destructor effects are an explicit boundary of this audit. The inspected resize shrinks the end pointer, or grows with zero-filled pointer entries when the saved count exceeds the current length. The dead target is not explicitly erased from the world creature map by this caller. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1965B0–0x196685; `decompiled/client/functions/0040d9d0.c`, RVA 0x00D9D0.]

## Relationship removal, kill records and completion rewards

The next world-map traversal processes each nonnull creature in native key order. A contact identifier matching either the hit's target key or the dead target's +0x11C8 identifier becomes zero. From that creature's threat map, every entry with either key is first collected into a temporary iterator list; only after the search finishes are those entries erased. The erase removes and frees each tree node and decreases a nonzero map count. There is no matching clear of +0x13AC contact counts and no wholesale resident-buff-list removal in this fatal continuation. This removal stage does not append zero-valued threat notifications. A zero linked identifier is still one of the compared keys. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1966A0–0x19682F; `decompiled/client/functions/0043ede0.c`, RVA 0x03EDE0; `binary/Cube.exe`, erase continuation bytes at RVAs 0x03F01A–0x03F038.]

After each creature's removals, a dead numeric-role-1 target and a current role-0 creature can produce a kill record. The requirement is that the dead target's **current** threat map contain the current creature-map entry key; the stored threat need not be positive. There is no recipient-health or distance check. The outgoing +0x48 record contains that recipient key, the hit target key, dead species and truncated reward. RVA 0x1A0BF0 is called immediately after append. Because these lookups use live maps during the same traversal that performs removals, eligibility is not an independent snapshot of the original threat map. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1967FD–0x1968C7; `decompiled/client/functions/005a0bf0.c`, RVA 0x1A0BF0.]

For the same dead-role-1/current-role-0 pairing, region completion grants two outgoing +0x40 item rewards **whether or not that creature qualified for a kill record**. The first has category 13 and a short +0x10 equal to truncation of 50 × C(region level + region rank) + 1. Region level is integer +0x3C and region rank byte +0x40 in the original region object. The second is generated by one parity draw: even selects RVA 0x1F51E0, odd RVA 0x1F8AD0, with the copied region level/rank and the recipient's character-class byte +0x140. Each reward is appended before any local insertion. A recipient pointer equal to world +0xB8 receives each item immediately through its inventory at +0x11DC using RVA 0x06EBE0 with group argument −1. The generator's random calls therefore occur between the first and second reward append for each recipient, and recipients are handled in world-map order. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1968C7–0x196AD8; `decompiled/client/functions/0046ebe0.c`, RVA 0x06EBE0.]

The inspected inventory helper converts category-13 +0x10 into an increment of its category-specific counter rather than a normal item slot. Other items can stack, fill an empty slot or extend the selected group. Group selection and stack-equivalence dependencies are not fully audited here; this is not a general inventory contract. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/0046ebe0.c`, RVA 0x06EBE0.]

RVA 0x1A0BF0 resolves both record identifiers again and requires both nonnull. When the victim pointer equals the local pointer, its linked creature, if found, receives health zero and its entire +0x13A4 threat map is cleared through RVA 0x27E480; the local victim's contact identifier is cleared. A local recipient gains the record's signed reward in +0x194 when the resolved victim has numeric role 1, followed by RVA 0x047B00. Independently, mode zero looks up the recipient's +0x11C8 link: a found linked creature with positive health gains the same signed reward and undergoes RVA 0x047B00. Its resulting +0x194 is copied into recipient +0x1024, its level's low short into +0x1030, and the embedded item at recipient +0x1020 is copied to recipient +0x1D48. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x1A0BF0–0x1A0D78 and 0x27E480–0x27E4C7.]

RVA 0x047B00 acts only with positive initial level. Each threshold is truncation of 1000 × C(current level) + 50. Sufficient accumulated +0x194 subtracts that threshold, increments level and replaces health with the result of RVA 0x044DB0; thresholds are recomputed until insufficient reward remains. This loop itself draws no random value. The full maximum-health dependency, including its item-stat calls at RVA 0x0C70B0, is not completed in this scope, so this contract identifies the exact health-query boundary rather than claiming a closed numerical health result or a complete nested stat/RNG audit. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x047B00–0x047C1D; `decompiled/client/functions/00444db0.c`, RVA 0x044DB0.]

Finally, cached zone coordinates must both be in 0 through 65535. Their quotient by 64 selects a region pointer from world +0xBC; their local remainder selects a zone pointer at region +0x10018. With the zone present and dead target +0x1B8 a nonnegative in-range vector index, the selected pointed record receives integer +0x38 = 1,200,000 and +0x3C = world +0x800160. There is no additional null test of that selected element. This timer reset follows the relationship/reward traversal, including its random draws. Later expiry or respawn behavior is outside this audit. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, RVA 0x195A60, final cached-zone section.]

The calculations use the native 32767 normalization constant and signed remainder where shown by the original instructions. This audit establishes the call order and inputs within these routines, not the global seeding or other callers of that random stream. A placement call's own draws occur before the caller continues to its next roll. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, RVAs 0x19B180–0x19C3D3, 0x19AEB0–0x19B178, 0x1F8530–0x1F858B; `binary/Cube.exe`, constant RVA 0x2FD338.]

Loot rejects numeric roles 0 and 3, and any dead target with word +0x7E bit 0x800, before any draw. An eligible invocation first consumes one unused draw. It collects pointers to nonempty equipment items in offset order +0x990, +0xAA8, +0x530, +0x878, +0x760, +0x648, +0x418. A nonempty collection consumes one remainder-by-4 draw; remainder zero or target flag 0x200 causes one selected equipment drop. The decompiler wrongly marks this branch unreachable; original assembly RVAs 0x19B33D–0x19B422 contains the executed branch and placement call. No source equipment item is cleared by this path. ## Nested generator and placement draws

All jitter pairs above are X then Y, each float 1 − 2 × draw / 32767, with Z zero. Each component is multiplied by float 65536 and converted to signed 64-bit integer through RVA 0x28D946 before addition to the dead target's signed fixed position.
