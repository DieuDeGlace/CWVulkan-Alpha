## Stored anchor and initialization

The world embedded at client `+0x2E4` owns a three-component binary32 anchor at world `+0x8000F0`, `+0x8000F4` and `+0x8000F8`, equivalently client `+0x8003D4` through `+0x8003DC`. The accessor at RVA `0x087FE0` returns this storage. Its components use ordinary world-coordinate units. A horizontal unit is one terrain-column spacing. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x087FE0`, `0x02C460`, `0x0347A0` and `0x09C6C0`.

The reset routine at RVA `0x1A52E0` assigns horizontal bits `0x4B002080` to both components and positive-zero bits to Z at `0x1A55F8`–`0x1A560C`. The horizontal value is exactly 8,396,928, also `0x802080`. The ordinary direct reset callers verified in the image are world construction at `0x18EE1D`, client startup at `0x05A741`, and the world-identity-change worker at `0x06AFAB`. Each reaches the same anchor assignment. The client-startup call takes its seed from the millisecond timer. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1A55F8`–`0x1A5616`, `0x18EE10`–`0x18EE22`, `0x05A72D`–`0x05A746` and `0x06AF96`–`0x06AFB0`.

World construction has a later initialization of its own. At `0x193A09`–`0x193A32` it assigns the same horizontal values and provisional Z equal to 100, bits `0x42C80000`. It then calls the native procedural height producer at `0x1C5E20` with those two horizontal integer coordinates and third argument zero, and stores the returned height as binary32 Z at `0x193A64`–`0x193A7C`. The embedded client world subsequently passes through the client-startup reset above, whose stored Z is zero. The procedural-height result belongs to the constructor phase, and a later reset establishes the zero-Z anchor again. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x193A09`–`0x193A7E`, `0x1C5E20` and `0x05A741`.

## World selection and refresh

The ordinary selection method at `0x06F620` records a requested seed at client `+0x800A50` and requested world-name value at `+0x800A54`. The selection action reaches it at `0x084209`. New-world completion creates the world record and reaches the same selection method at `0x082939`, followed by the world-list refresh at `0x082940`. The refresh routine at `0x0A23D0` rebuilds list presentation from world records. The anchor assignment occurs through the world reset reached by the worker after an identity change. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x06F6E0`–`0x06F700`, `0x084170`–`0x08422A`, `0x082530`–`0x082945` and `0x0A23D0`.

The worker at `0x06A8A0` compares that requested seed with the current world's seed at client `+0x800448`, and compares the requested name with the current world's name at client `+0x378`. A differing seed or name admits world teardown and reset, including the anchor reset at `0x06AFAB`. Matching identity continues the resident-terrain refresh path with the stored anchor preserved. The worker runs in a separate native thread created during startup at `0x0635BF`–`0x06360C`. Its ordinary loop requests a 20-millisecond sleep at `0x06B6A3`, performs cleanup, and repeats while client byte `+0x8005B0` is nonzero at `0x06B708`–`0x06B70F`. Work and scheduling contribute to its actual period. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x06A8D6`–`0x06A971`, `0x06AF74`–`0x06AFB0`, `0x0635BF`–`0x06360C`, `0x050E70` and `0x06B6A3`–`0x06B715`.

## Frame admission and terrain height

The menu-anchor branch is admitted when the selected display value is nonzero for any of four client root pointers, `+0x800874` for the start root, `+0x800888` for character selection, `+0x800894` for the server-address screen, or `+0x80088C` for world selection. The selected value is a 32-bit entry in the root's property storage, selected through root `+0x3C`, descriptor `+0x68` and storage pointer `+0x94`. The predicate at `0x07FA10` tests that value against zero. The four admission checks precede the ordinary actor-follow path. The character-creation root at `+0x800880` and new-world-entry root at `+0x800890` independently reach the actor-follow vertical branch. A simultaneously admitted menu root takes the earlier menu-anchor branch. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x09C3C4`–`0x09C410`, `0x09C4FF`–`0x09C525`, `0x09C682`–`0x09C6C0`, `0x07FA10`, `0x083E70`, `0x0815E0`, `0x082530` and `0x084170`.

On each admitted update, the entire stored binary32 anchor is converted into the camera's three signed 64-bit coordinates at client `+0x158` through `+0x168`. Each source component is first multiplied by binary32 65,536, constant bits `0x47800000` at image RVA `0x2FCE7C`, and then converted to a signed integer by the native conversion helper. Thus these coordinates have sixteen fractional bits. The initialized horizontal components convert exactly to 550,301,073,408 each. The consumer derives its lookup X and Y by signed integer division of the converted coordinates by 65,536. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x09C6C0`–`0x09C709`, `0x02C460`–`0x02C49E`, `0x02F570` and `0x28D946`.

The lookup uses the live world-terrain table at world `+0xBC`. Valid horizontal column coordinates are nonnegative and strictly below 16,777,216. Each 256-by-256 zone belongs to a 64-by-64-zone region, and the 1,024-by-1,024 region table is indexed with X outermost and Y innermost. A resident region supplies its zone-pointer table at `+0x10018`, likewise X outermost. A resident zone supplies column storage through `+0xA8`, with 32-byte column records and Y outermost within the 256-by-256 columns. The default anchor therefore selects zone coordinates 32,800 and 32,800, local column coordinates 128 and 128, in region coordinates 512 and 512. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x0347A0`–`0x03486E`, `0x034D10`–`0x034D86` and `0x034A90`–`0x034AC4`.

A returned column supplies two 32-bit integers, its vertical base at `+0x10` and its stored element count at `+0x1C`. Count-based block indexing is independently visible at `0x19AAF0` and `0x1AF455`–`0x1AF50D`. The menu focus replaces Z with their 32-bit sum converted to the signed sixteen-fractional-bit coordinate representation. This is the boundary immediately above the stored vertical extent. The menu consumer uses those two fields directly, including for a zero count. Its height dependency is the stored extent. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x09C715`–`0x09C745`, `0x0348B0`, `0x034B70`, `0x012080`, `0x19AAF0` and `0x1AF455`–`0x1AF50D`.

The final vertical addition is exactly 50 whole world units, represented by the signed fixed-point value 3,276,800. A missing region, zone or valid column leaves the freshly copied anchor Z as the input to that addition. Consequently an ordinary post-reset anchor with absent terrain gives focus Z equal to 50. A present column gives focus Z equal to its stored vertical base plus count plus 50. The anchor is copied again on the next admitted update, so the lift has no frame-to-frame accumulation. Resident-column admission or extent changes can change the focus height on the next admitted update. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x09C6C0`–`0x09C76F`, `0x012080` and `0x02C9B0`.

The main frame callback invokes client update through its virtual slot at `0x0C8633`, before the draw callback at `0x0C86EC` and presentation at `0x0C86FF`. The supported client's update slot contains RVA `0x088EE0`, recorded at image RVA `0x2FFDF0`. The menu-focus block executes once on its admitted path through that update, outside the refresh worker and without an enclosing repeated simulation-step loop. Its replacement and 50-unit addition use the current anchor and current resident column on that frame. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x0C860D`–`0x0C8702`, `0x088EE0`–`0x09D910`, `0x2FFDC8`–`0x2FFDF8` and `0x09C6C0`–`0x09C76F`.

## Controlled actor and remaining limits

The controlled actor has a separate position triple at actor `+0x10` through `+0x24`. Startup explicitly converts the then-current world anchor into that triple at `0x062042`–`0x06205E`. On a world identity change, the worker resolves the actor's saved position for the requested seed-and-name identity. A missing saved entry selects the then-current anchor and converts it into actor position at `0x06B54D`–`0x06B592`. A present entry supplies the saved signed 64-bit coordinate triple at `0x06B597`–`0x06B604`. The worker then synchronizes the retained drawable-position triple at actor `+0x1350` through `+0x1364` and clears the three binary32 velocity components at `+0x34` through `+0x3C`. These actor copies use the stored anchor or saved position. The 50-unit lift belongs to the frame's camera-focus calculation. Evidence `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x062042`–`0x06205E` and `0x06B3C2`–`0x06B686`.

The anchor's ordinary constructor and reset writes, their direct callers, and the accessor's ordinary consumers were traced. Computed aliases outside these ordinary paths remain an evidence limit. Exact first-present terrain height depends on which native columns are resident and their live base/count values. Actor simulation between startup, identity changes and character creation can further change actor position. Procedural generation's complete numeric output, nonstandard floating-point controls, exceptional allocation or loading failures, concurrent observation during teardown, and runtime scheduling are outside this bounded contract.
