# Alpha world-hit fatal effects

Status: FINAL / STABLE / HELD after complete author readback. This independent contract covers the fatal continuation of client world-hit application, its immediate state and output effects, and bounded loot, numeric adjustment and credit dependencies. It does not specify later corpse disposal or respawn processing.

## Evidence and application boundary

The supported original image is `binary/Cube.exe`, SHA-256 `84a7a132a84d4282338e7ea45a1940d32066d64e39cbafed8f2418d5a6dc30bf`, preferred image base `0x00400000`. Addresses below are RVAs. Evidence paths are relative to `Documents/disassembled alpha cube world`. The main evidence is `decompiled/client/functions/00595a60.c`, `00596d30.c`, `0059b180.c`, the individually cited original helpers, and `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`. Scoped native disassembly resolves missing or misleading decompiler branches. No reconstruction, implementation material, runtime findings or earlier held contract was used.

World application helper `0x195A60` receives world in ECX and three stack arguments: a hit-record pointer, a mutable output aggregate, and a caller-owned collection used by nested drop publication. Normal return removes twelve argument bytes. Denote the record by R, aggregate by A and collection by C. The helper looks up R’s eight-byte target key at `+0x08` in world `+0x04`; its mapped pointer is the target T used by the continuation. R’s source key is at `+0x00`, binary32 amount at `+0x10`, and effect selector at `+0x45`. [Evidence: native entry `0x195A8E–0x195AC7`; `00595a60.c`.]

The target lookup must find a nonnull pointer. An initial comparison skips ordered health at most zero; unordered health can pass this initial comparison if floating-point execution completes. When world `+0xB8` is null and T’s byte `+0x60` is zero, this helper appends the hit to A `+0x00` and exits through its forwarding path. The remaining application path resolves the source key separately. A missing source does not by itself exclude every later application branch. [Evidence: native/discovered assembly `0x195ACC–0x195B3E`.]

Several conditions precede fatal admission: tracking/relationship work, the target flag `0x2000` relationship gate, effect-specific work, and the health-ownership gate requiring world byte `+0xB4` zero or T equal to world `+0xB8`. Effect selectors one, three and four have paths that bypass this health/fatal continuation. Positive ordinary damage can subtract R’s binary32 amount, subject to helper `0x03E310` on target and any resolved source; effect five instead has a resident-type-six absorption path. Negative amounts have a healing/capping path. These upstream producers, predicates and all their side effects are bounded here rather than replaced by a universal “positive hit kills” rule. The fatal comparison is reached at `0x196133`: binary32 zero is compared with current T health, and carry branches away. Consequently the fatal body requires ordered health at most zero; unordered health does not enter it. [Evidence: `00595a60.c`; assembly `0x195CE5–0x196143`, especially `0x195ED2–0x196052` and `0x19613C–0x196143`.]

The target pointer, its own ID and the map entry key are separate inputs. Application uses the pointer obtained by looking up R’s target key, without checking equality with T’s own ID at `+0x08/+0x0C`. Generic hit construction in `0x196D30` normally copies supplied source/target creatures’ own IDs into a record, appends the record, and calls `0x195A60` synchronously when world `+0xB4` is zero. A null source produces an all-zero source ID. That producer does not eliminate the distinction at the later lookup boundary. Queued records can also be supplied by the world routine. [Evidence: `00596d30.c`, final record construction and call at `0x1986D2`; `00595a60.c`; world `0x20CD80–0x20CD9B` in `decompiled/client/assembly-functions/0060c510.asm`.]

## Immediate fatal order

The following ordering is synchronous inside `0x195A60`, subject to the stated nested calls completing. Allocation or nested failure is not rolled back by an established transaction.

1. T health `+0x16C` is written as four zero bytes. A position is obtained from T’s three signed 64-bit coordinates beginning at `+0x10`, converted to binary32 coordinates and scaled by exact binary32 `1/65536` through `0x02C4A0`. An effect is appended to A `+0x08`, with that position, 32-bit selector `0x1B`, and two binary32 values equal to one. T’s 32-bit action timer `+0x6C` is then set to zero. The append precedes the timer store. No replacement value for action byte `+0x68` is written by this particular sequence. [Evidence: `0x196149–0x19618C`; `decompiled/client/functions/0042c4a0.c`, `004864c0.c`.]

2. World lookup `0x034D10` receives T’s signed 32-bit fields `+0x1B0/+0x1B4`. If found, the routine scans its `0x188`-byte object records in vector order. The first record with 32-bit kind five, record `+0x178` equal to T `+0x1B8`, and nonzero record byte `+0x30` is handled. Helper `0x1A58E0` is called with requested byte state zero and A; a state snapshot is then appended to A `+0x30`, carrying the two supplied coordinates, the object-vector index, and copied object fields. The scan stops at that match. [Evidence: `0x196193–0x196219`; `00468670.c`, `004dd700.c`.]

3. World loot helper `0x19B180` is called with T and C. Its admitted drop construction and random operations happen before the later relationship and credit work. This call occurs even when that helper’s own eligibility checks cause an immediate return. [Evidence: `0x196221–0x19622E`; `0059b180.c`.]

4. The world creature map is traversed; for each nonnull mapping whose creature byte `+0x60` is zero, a fixed-coordinate difference from T is calculated into a temporary through `0x02C7A0`. The checked helper writes that temporary; this loop does not establish a distance-based recipient filter. Next, T’s eight-byte link at `+0x11C8` is considered. If nonzero and it resolves to a nonnull mapped creature, that creature’s health `+0x16C` is set to zero. This is a direct health store, with no recursive call to this fatal body and no corresponding second loot/effect sequence established here. [Evidence: `0x196233–0x1962DC`; `0042c7a0.c`, `00477d90.c`.]

5. A common credit amount is calculated, then optional regional progress/completion work runs. Afterward the world creature map is traversed for relationship cleanup and per-recipient credit/rewards. The final backing-record writes occur after that traversal. These stages are detailed below. [Evidence: `0x1962E6–0x196C49`.]

Helper `0x1A58E0` immediately changes the matching object when its byte state differs from the requested state: its signed 32-bit field `+0x34` becomes `1000` minus the old value, clamped to zero when negative, then byte `+0x30` receives the new state. For the kind-five state-zero call above it also appends effect selector `0x33` to A `+0x08`, with position converted from the object’s fixed triplet beginning at `+0x08` and two binary32 ones. The following snapshot append observes the changed fields. The snapshot copier selects documented scalar fields and does not establish initialized padding or an all-zero record. [Evidence: `decompiled/client/functions/005a58e0.c`, `00468670.c`; fatal call `0x1961E9`.]

## Regional progress, relationships and final backing record

Regional lookup is admitted only when world `+0xB4` is zero. T’s signed fields `+0x1B0/+0x1B4` are divided by eight with truncation toward zero and supplied to `0x087DA0`. The returned object G must exist and its byte `+0x41` must differ from two. T must have word flag `0x2000`, or G’s 32-bit selector `+0x34` must be five and T byte `+0x1D38` nonzero. T’s relationship map `+0x13A4` is then searched in native traversal order for an entry with ordered-positive binary32 value whose key resolves to a nonnull world creature with byte `+0x60` zero. The first such entry admits the following selector decision. A positive contribution is required here; mere key presence is insufficient. [Evidence: `0x19639A–0x1964FA`; `00595a60.c`.]

G selectors one through five and thirteen set completion byte `+0x41` to two and the local completion flag to one. Selectors seven through twelve first increment G’s 32-bit progress `+0x44`, store state byte one, and then set state two/completion when the updated signed progress is at least G’s signed goal `+0x48`. Other selectors do not establish these progress effects. The changed descriptor beginning at G `+0x2C` is copied into an event appended to A `+0x60`; its first two fields are T’s two signed coordinates divided by eight toward zero, and its descriptor begins at event `+0x10`. Original constructors/copy helpers leave padding limits; no all-bytes-zero claim is made. [Evidence: native switch at `0x1964FA`, progress stores `0x196501–0x196517`, append `0x196579`; `00468620.c`, `00465c70.c`, `00486420.c`.]

When the local completion flag is set, the routine visits the corresponding eight-by-eight range of spatial coordinates, with X outermost and Y innermost. Each base coordinate is the signed quotient by eight, truncated toward zero, multiplied by eight. For a found spatial object, its pointer vector at `+0x18` is processed from signed index `+0xA0` through its current end: nonnull entries receive their virtual destruction call with argument one, then the vector is shortened to the retained prefix. This happens inside fatal application, before global relationship cleanup. Spatial lookup, object destruction and vector failure behavior are bounded dependencies. [Evidence: `0x19658B–0x19667D`; `00595a60.c`.]

The subsequent world-map traversal uses each current mapped pointer P and its map entry key independently. For nonnull P, its link `+0x11D0/+0x11D4` is cleared, lower word first, when it equals either R’s target key or T’s `+0x11C8` link. Comparisons are integer word comparisons, despite misleading floating-point pointer types in the decompile. Entries in P’s relationship map `+0x13A4` with either of those two keys are collected and erased after scanning that relationship map. These are relationship-map erasures; this loop does not erase the world’s creature-map entry. T’s link remains the comparison source; its zero value is not globally excluded from these equality tests. [Evidence: native/discovered assembly `0x1966A0–0x196821`; `0043ede0.c`.]

After all recipients have been visited, T’s coordinates `+0x1B0/+0x1B4` must be nonnegative and below `65,536`. The routine resolves their region coordinates by signed division by sixty-four, requires each region coordinate below `1024` and a nonnull region pointer, then uses the local coordinate remainders to resolve the corresponding spatial object. T’s signed index `+0x1B8` must be nonnegative and within that object’s pointer vector at `+0x18`. The selected pointed-to record receives 32-bit `1,200,000` at `+0x38`, followed by the current world `+0x800160` word at `+0x3C`. The element pointer is used without an additional null test at this final store site. These are immediate backing-record writes; their later timer/period consumers and respawn meaning are not specified here. [Evidence: `0x196B5F–0x196C49`.]

## Credit calculation and synchronous consumers

The scalar helper `0x03CA60` starts with a binary32 input, subtracts one, multiplies by binary32 `0.05000000074505806` (bits `0x3D4CCCCD`), adds one, divides one by that result, then subtracts the quotient from one. Each arithmetic operation is scalar binary32. Its stored binary32 result is returned through x87. Denote that rounded result by F for the input in question; this notation is mathematical description only. [Evidence: `0043ca60.c`; native `0x03CA60–0x03CA94`.]

For fatal credit, T’s signed 32-bit field `+0x190` is added to its zero-extended byte `+0x1A8` with 32-bit arithmetic. The result is converted to binary32 and passed to `0x03CA60`; the returned value is narrowed to binary32, multiplied by `20` (bits `0x41A00000`), and replaced by binary32 one only when it is ordered below one. Predicate `0x0445D0` true multiplies the result by twenty again. That predicate recognizes T’s 32-bit kinds `0x65`, `0x6B`, `0x6C`, `0x6D`, `0x72`, `0x73`, `0x74`, `0x75`, `0x76`, `0x77`. Word flag `0x200` then multiplies by ten (bits `0x41200000`); flag `0x800` finally forces binary32 zero. There is no second lower bound after these adjustments. [Evidence: `0x1962E6–0x196385`; `004445d0.c`; original binary constants at RVAs `0x2FCD74`, `0x345E74`, `0x345DC0`.]

During the world-map cleanup traversal, ordinary XP credit is considered only when T byte `+0x60` is one and P byte `+0x60` is zero. T’s relationship map must contain P’s map entry key; this particular credit test does not re-test the contribution’s sign or magnitude. A 24-byte record is appended to A `+0x48`: recipient map key at `+0x00`, R’s target key at `+0x08`, T’s 32-bit kind at `+0x10`, and the common credit at `+0x14` converted from binary32 to signed 32-bit using truncation toward zero. The record is then passed immediately to world consumer `0x1A0BF0`. A zero computed credit does not by itself suppress record production. [Evidence: `0x196824–0x1968E8`; `00451d00.c`.]

Consumer `0x1A0BF0` resolves both record IDs through the world map and requires both pointers nonnull. If the second pointer equals world `+0xB8`, its linked creature, when resolvable, has health zeroed and its `+0x13A4` relationship map cleared; world `+0xB8` then has its `+0x11D0` link cleared. If the recipient equals nonnull world `+0xB8` and the second creature has byte `+0x60` one, the signed 32-bit credit is added to recipient `+0x194`, then `0x047B00` runs on that recipient. With world `+0xB4` zero, a resolved, ordered-living linked creature of the recipient also receives the same credit and the same helper call; its resulting `+0x194` is copied to recipient item field `+0x1024`, its low level word at `+0x190` to recipient `+0x1030`, and item state is copied onward through `0x02C5E0`. These effects are synchronous. [Evidence: `005a0bf0.c`; native/discovered assembly `0x1A0BF0–0x1A0D78`.]

Helper `0x047B00` can consume accumulated `+0x194`, increment level `+0x190`, and set current health from maximum-health helper `0x044DB0`, repeatedly while its level threshold admits another increment. Its threshold rounding, maximum-health dependencies, overflow limits and exceptional cases are not fully closed by this contract. Thus credit application can already include level/health changes before the fatal caller returns; the queued credit record is not the sole effect. [Evidence: `00447b00.c`; calls at `0x1A0CF2`, `0x1A0D41`.]

## Completion grants and their order

When T byte `+0x60` is one, P byte `+0x60` zero and the local regional completion flag is set, two grant records are appended to A `+0x40`. This completion gate is reached even when the preceding XP relationship-key test failed. The first record has recipient P’s map entry key, item category byte thirteen and a quantity word at item `+0x10`. The quantity is obtained by adding the copied regional descriptor’s signed 32-bit `+0x10` and zero-extended byte `+0x14`, applying `0x03CA60`, narrowing to binary32, multiplying by fifty (bits `0x42480000`), adding one, truncating to signed 32-bit, then storing the low sixteen bits. There is no explicit quantity clamp in this construction. [Evidence: `0x1968ED–0x1969F7`; `00451cc0.c`.]

If P equals world `+0xB8`, that first item is immediately supplied to `0x06EBE0` on P’s inventory object at `+0x11DC`, with selection argument minus one. The checked category-thirteen branch adds its signed 16-bit quantity to the inventory object’s 32-bit counter at `+0x12C`. A remote recipient has the emitted grant here without this local-pointer branch.

Next a direct imported `rand` draw chooses between item generators `0x1F51E0` and `0x1F8AD0` by signed remainder modulo two. Their inputs include the copied descriptor’s signed numeric field and byte, and P’s byte `+0x140`. The generated item replaces the temporary item, then a second grant record is appended for the same recipient key. The same local-inventory call occurs afterward when P equals world `+0xB8`. The complete generated item, generator draws and general inventory stacking/selection behavior remain unclosed. Grant append, optional local grant, random selection/generation, second append, and optional second local grant occur in that order for each recipient. [Evidence: `0x1969F9–0x196AD3`; `0046ebe0.c`.]

## Loot helper, item adjustment and random ordering

Loot helper `0x19B180` returns before any draw when T byte `+0x60` is zero or three, or word flag `0x800` is set. Otherwise it first consumes one imported `rand` result whose value is unused, then builds a temporary list of nonempty item pointers in this order: T `+0x990`, `+0xAA8`, `+0x530`, `+0x878`, `+0x760`, `+0x648`, `+0x418`. Nonempty here is a nonzero category byte. These items are referenced for copying; this collection step does not clear their source slots. [Evidence: `0059b180.c`; native `0x19B1C0–0x19B326`; `0066add0.c`.]

If that pointer list is nonempty, a further draw is tested by signed remainder modulo four. Remainder zero, or T flag `0x200`, admits an equipped-item drop; the draw is consumed even when the flag admits it. The admitted branch draws two horizontal offsets in order, constructs their displacement, draws an item-list index by unsigned remainder over the nonzero list size, then draws a heading and calls drop emitter `0x19AEB0` with the selected original item pointer. This native branch is omitted as unreachable in the original decompile and is confirmed directly in the supported binary. [Evidence: `0x19B326–0x19B422`.]

Following that branch, the direct outer loot stages occur in this order:

- For T byte `+0x60` one or T kind `0x8F`, a draw is tested by signed remainder modulo ten. On admission, the generated-item numeric level is T’s signed `+0x190` minus one plus a draw’s signed remainder modulo two, bounded below by one; another parity draw then selects `0x1F51E0` or `0x1F8AD0`. Material/quality helper `0x1F8530` and the selected generator are nested dependencies. The generated temporary item is adjusted through `0x0C7E80` with binary32 radius bits `0x3D4CCCCD` and byte argument one, then position and heading draws precede a drop. A subsequent remainder-by-five draw can admit a second item through `0x1F79E0`, with the same adjustment and placement pattern. That second gate is inside the first admitted branch.
- A direct draw supplies signed remainder by three plus one for the kind-specific material-item stage. Item selection depends on T’s 32-bit kind; kinds `0x78`, `0x79`, `0x7C`, `0x7E` make an additional parity choice. Constructed nonzero items use position/heading draws and the same drop emitter. The zero-item default leaves this stage. The complete kind-to-item/material table remains a bounded dependency here.
- For byte `+0x60` one or kind `0x8F`, a remainder-by-fifty draw can admit a bonus item through `0x1F6D50` and `0x1F8530`, with placement/heading draws. The following branch on word flags `0x18` selects an ordinary currency calculation using helper `0x045F60`, or a category-fourteen construction. The ordinary branch uses a random multiplier before splitting the truncated result into base-one-hundred denominations and emitting nonzero components. The category-fourteen branch uses a remainder-by-one-thousand draw plus twenty times byte `+0x1A8` to choose a numeric tier, with thresholds `700`, `950`, `999`; flag `0x200` increments that tier capped at three. A remainder-by-twenty draw and nonzero tier gate its item construction, including another draw stored in the item seed. The ordinary amount helper and complete item semantics remain unresolved.
- Finally, T byte `+0x60` one admits traversal of its inventory vectors. An entry’s signed 32-bit raw count must be positive and its item category nonzero. Category one/subcategory one is excluded. Other admitted entries make one drop attempt per raw count, in vector/entry/repetition order, with two offset draws and a heading draw before each emitter call. No source inventory erase is established by this loop.

These are draw-order boundaries, not probability or total-draw-count claims: nested generators, amount helpers and the emitter can consume additional draws. [Evidence: `0059b180.c`, `0x19B427–0x19C3B8`; native equipped-item branch above.]

The direct horizontal draw arithmetic uses binary32 one minus the result of converting the signed draw to binary32, multiplying by two and dividing by `32767` (bits `0x46FFFE00`). Heading uses binary32 draw times `360` (bits `0x43B40000`) divided by `32767`. Fixed-displacement conversion and the imported floating-to-integer helper `0x28D946` are retained as a rounding/control dependency where not independently closed. The native import’s random-state algorithm, distribution and interaction with other callers are not specified.

Drop emitter `0x19AEB0` first makes a lifetime draw, storing signed remainder by `300` plus `500`, and records world `+0x800160`. It resolves the supplied fixed X/Y position to a spatial object. If lookup succeeds, it copies the item and consumes another heading draw, replacing the supplied heading while still preserving the caller’s earlier draw consumption. It searches downward for a supporting cell using game terrain helpers, sets fixed Z to the next integer position above that support, immediately appends the dropped-item state to the spatial object’s vector at `+0x30`, and adds that spatial object pointer to C through `0x18E7D0`. Thus world drop state is already changed and C records an object for a later caller-owned publication opportunity. Terrain termination, invalid cells and C’s eventual snapshot/transport consumer remain unclosed. [Evidence: `0059aeb0.c`; native `0x19AEB0–0x19AF9A`, `0x19B11F–0x19B163`; `0044a970.c`, `0044d590.c`, `0058e7d0.c`.]

The item adjustment at `0x0C7E80` concerns the item’s signed 16-bit field `+0x10`, not a victim price or a damage scalar. Values below two are left unchanged. For a larger value L, the helper evaluates F(L) and subtracts the supplied binary32 radius. If the result is at most zero, it stores one without a draw. Otherwise inverse helper `0x0462F0` converts that lower value back to a numeric level, narrows to binary32, truncates to signed 32-bit, and bounds the lower endpoint to at least one. With the supplied byte argument one, the binary32 sum of F(L) and the radius is capped at bits `0x3F7FF972`; inverse conversion of that capped input, binary32 narrowing and signed truncation supply the upper endpoint. One draw then selects by signed remainder over upper minus lower plus one, and the resulting low sixteen bits replace item `+0x10`. The inverse performs one divided by one minus input, subtracts one, multiplies by twenty, then adds one, in scalar binary32 before its x87 return. This closes the immediate adjustment and conditional draw, without assigning an unproved economic or adaptation-policy meaning. [Evidence: `004c7e80.c`, `004462f0.c`; native `0x0C7E80–0x0C7F5C`, `0x0462F0–0x046323`.]

## Completion and explicit limits

Health clamping, timer reset, object state changes, spawned drop storage, linked-health stores, regional progress/destruction, relationship cleanup, local XP/level-helper calls, local grants and backing-record stores described above happen before `0x195A60` returns. Appended aggregate effects, object/progress snapshots, XP records, grant records and C’s changed-object entries are outputs for their callers and later consumers; their creation does not prove rendering, packet transmission, acknowledgement, durable storage or exactly-once remote application. The main fatal continuation does not erase T’s world-map entry or itself establish a complete corpse/respawn lifecycle.

Unclosed dependencies include upstream hit/scalar producers and predicates; complete world-object and regional descriptor producers; terrain and drop placement failure/termination; all generated item/material details; the ordinary currency amount helper; level thresholds and maximum-health internals; general inventory grant policy; imported random/floating conversion behavior; callbacks, allocation failures and synchronization outside the documented calls. Numeric operations retain native binary32 narrowing, 32-bit wrap and low-word stores rather than adding unstated clamps. Exceptional floating-point inputs, invalid pointers/indices, integer division faults and ambient SSE/x87 controls are not certified safe by this contract. No server counterpart or server/client equivalence is inferred.
