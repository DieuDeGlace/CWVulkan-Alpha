# Original client descriptor stock generation

Status: FINAL / STABLE / HELD.

The supported image is `binary/Cube.exe`, SHA-256 `84a7a132a84d4282338e7ea45a1940d32066d64e39cbafed8f2418d5a6dc30bf`, image base `0x00400000`. All addresses below are RVAs. Evidence paths are relative to the original evidence root, `Documents/disassembled alpha cube world`. This contract was independently derived from the original image and the individually identified original files cited below. It uses no reconstruction, replacement implementation, runtime observations or earlier research contract.

The closed stock-producing body is RVA `0x1C9B30`, together with its two reached item selectors, `0x1F51E0` and `0x1F8AD0`, and the ordinary successful insertion boundary at `0x06EBE0`. The upstream descriptor branch is bounded to the verified calls and gates below. This is one concrete stock route; it does not establish the complete NPC, vendor, crafting or item-generation system. The original decompilation omits important local-container behavior in both selectors: the actual image supplies a finite candidate count for their final selection, rather than the apparent zero divisor in the decompiled text. Native instructions control those findings. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9B30–0x1C9F68`, `0x1F51E0–0x1F56B5`, `0x1F8AD0–0x1F8E01`; `decompiled/client/functions/005c9b30.c`, `005f51e0.c`, `005f8ad0.c`; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, corresponding addresses.]

## Upstream admission, descriptor identity and publication

Routine `0x1E4850` calls `0x1AF170` at `0x1E9672` when the supplied second object's 32-bit field `+0x18` equals 1 or 5. Within `0x1AF170`, the stock-bearing branch described here is reached under that same supplied object's `+0x18 == 1` branch. The value 5 is therefore sufficient for the outer call, but does not establish admission to this particular stock route. The earlier production and meaning of those two objects remain outside this contract. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1E9655–0x1E9677`, `0x1AF1A7–0x1AF1C3`, `0x1BC9AC–0x1BC9BE`; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, functions at VA `005e4850` and `005af170`.]

The supplied first object has a collection at `+0x88`. The surrounding traversal obtains a pointer-valued entry and examines the pointed object: its collection at `+0x24` must be nonempty, its 32-bit discriminator at `+0x60` must equal 2, and its collection at `+0x3C` must be nonempty. These are distinct fields of that pointed object, not creature offsets. Empty tests compare the collection's first two pointer words. Other discriminator branches exist and are not included here. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1BCB62–0x1BCBCA`, `0x1BCD8E–0x1BCDA1`; `decompiled/client/functions/00477240.c`, RVA `0x077240`; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, same intervals.]

On this branch a new `0x10F0`-byte descriptor is allocated and constructed by `0x1ADB20`. A random draw selects an element from the pointed object's nonempty `+0x3C` collection using unsigned division/remainder by its element count; the selected value is passed to `0x1A6A70` for the descriptor's `+0x10` position storage. Afterward, descriptor `+0x28` is written as a 32-bit 3, a second random draw supplies signed remainder modulo 2 and then addition of 2 for descriptor `+0x2C`, descriptor `+0x34` receives the second supplied object's 32-bit `+0x24`, and descriptor byte `+0x30` becomes `0x80`. The constructor had zeroed the word at `+0x30`, so byte `+0x31` stays zero here. Position selection and this descriptor-kind draw precede all stock draws. Coordinate-service internals and the larger spatial generation are not closed. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1BCDA7–0x1BCE53`, `0x1ADB20–0x1ADB44`; `decompiled/client/functions/005adb20.c`; `decompiled/client/assembly.txt`, corresponding addresses.]

The call at `0x1BCE53` supplies the new descriptor as the first stack argument to `0x1C9B30`, plus two further 32-bit stack arguments, and forwards the enclosing receiver in ECX. The stock body reads the descriptor argument, saves that receiver and forwards it to the two item selectors; neither selector reads the forwarded receiver. The two further stack arguments are not read by this body. Descriptor level, numeric role, kind, class byte and specialization byte are not consulted by this stock body. In particular, descriptor `+0x34` does not determine its item-level bands. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1BCE2A–0x1BCE53`, `0x1C9B30–0x1C9F68`, `0x1F51E0–0x1F56B5`, `0x1F8AD0–0x1F8E01`.]

The descriptor's inventory begins at descriptor `+0xF6C`, established by the constructor's explicit receiver adjustment before `0x03C020`. Its three outer collection pointers, scalar `+0x0C`, semantic item members at inventory `+0x10`, and two 32-bit counters `+0x128/+0x12C` are initialized independently. The outer collection starts empty; the embedded item has category/subtype zero, level word 1, its other assigned semantic members zero, and its 256-byte extension area zero. This does not establish all-zero allocation or all-zero padding. Every stock insertion below explicitly selects inventory group 0. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1ADBC1–0x1ADBFE`, `0x03C020–0x03C099`, `0x1C9C28–0x1C9C59`, `0x1C9E91–0x1C9EB3`, `0x1C9F15–0x1C9F3D`; `decompiled/client/functions/0043c020.c`.]

Stock construction returns before the enclosing branch finishes assembling the descriptor's behavior object. The same descriptor pointer retained before the stock call is appended to the supplied first object's `+0x18` pointer collection at `0x1BD527`; this is pointer publication, not a second stock-generation call. Later, the separately verified actor-creation route in world routine `0x20C510` constructs an actor using descriptor `+0x48`, copies descriptor level to actor `+0x190`, the low byte of descriptor `+0x28` to actor `+0x60`, descriptor `+0x2C` to actor `+0x64`, and descriptor bytes `+0x30/+0x31` to actor `+0x140/+0x141`. It assigns descriptor inventory `+0xF6C` into actor inventory `+0x11DC` at `0x2103B3`. This proves the descriptor/actor storage relationship; the full admission, identity allocation, timing and retention of that later route remain outside scope. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1BCDC5–0x1BCE53`, `0x1BCF65`, `0x1BD4F9–0x1BD52C`, `0x20FFB4–0x210049`, `0x2103A6–0x2103BF`; `decompiled/client/functions/0066add0.c`, RVA `0x26ADD0`; `decompiled/client/functions/0044aff0.c`, RVA `0x04AFF0`.]

## Item members and level bands

The stock body constructs and reuses a local item with a `0x118`-byte storage stride. The semantic members copied by `0x02C5E0` and `0x00EE70` are the following. The role labels identify their use in this bounded route; the complete interpretation of every category, subtype, material and extension member is not established here.

| Item-relative member | Width | Established behavior in this route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+0x00`, `+0x01` | One byte each | Category and subtype selectors |
| `+0x04` | 32 bits | Zero for fixed and category-1 stock; a fresh random remainder modulo 100 for each generated candidate |
| `+0x08` | 32 bits | Zero initially; generated category-2 stock receives the selected candidate's original category, zero-extended |
| `+0x0C` | One byte | Supplied tier, or the fixed values below |
| `+0x0D` | One byte | Material selector, with the exact values below |
| `+0x0E` | One byte | Zero |
| `+0x10` | 16 bits | Item-level word, including truncation described below |
| `+0x14` through `+0x113` | 256 bytes | Explicitly zeroed; copied as 32 complete eight-byte entries |
| `+0x114` | 32 bits | Zero extension count |

Item bytes `+0x02/+0x03`, `+0x0F` and `+0x12/+0x13` are not initialized by these semantic constructors and are not written by the two item-copy services. Their values are unspecified by this route. The eight-byte entries inside the explicitly cleared extension area are all copied, even though the extension count is zero. Neither item-copy service is a complete `0x118`-byte raw copy. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9BE6–0x1C9C59`, `0x1C9DEF–0x1C9E42`, `0x1C9ED7–0x1C9F3D`, `0x02C5E0–0x02C672`, `0x00EE70–0x00EF10`; `decompiled/client/functions/0042c5e0.c`, `0040ee70.c`.]

The outer band index is a signed 32-bit integer, starting at 0 and advancing through 29 in ascending order. For each band, its integer index is converted to binary32 and divided by binary32 30, bit pattern `0x41F00000`. A second input first adds binary32 `0x3F7FFF58`, exactly `0.999989986419677734375`, to the converted index, then divides by the same 30. Each input is independently passed to scalar helper `0x0462F0`. That helper subtracts the input from binary32 1, divides binary32 1 by that difference, subtracts 1, multiplies by binary32 20, and adds 1, in that order. Every arithmetic instruction is scalar binary32; the result is stored as binary32 and loaded for the x87 return. The constants 1 and 20 have patterns `0x3F800000` and `0x41A00000`. The upper input uses the stated near-one addend, not exact 1. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9B63–0x1C9BAC`, `0x0462F0–0x046323`; original constants at RVAs `0x2FCD78`, `0x31DD4C`, `0x345DC0`, `0x2FCD74`; `decompiled/client/functions/004462f0.c`.]

Each returned value is immediately stored to binary32 again and converted to a signed 32-bit integer with truncation toward zero. The first integer is the lower endpoint and the second is the upper endpoint. One random draw follows both conversions. Signed 32-bit subtraction of lower from upper, then addition of 1, supplies the divisor for signed remainder of that draw. That remainder is added to the lower endpoint. A signed minimum of 1 is imposed on the result. At band 0 the result is subsequently forced to 1 even though both helper calls, both integer conversions and the random draw have already happened. No maximum-level clamp appears. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9B7D–0x1C9BFA`; `decompiled/client/functions/005c9b30.c`.]

The band result remains a 32-bit integer until item production. Both item selectors take only its low 16 bits for item `+0x10`; the final category-1 item also stores only that low word. There is no saturation or signed-word range check at those stores. This matters particularly at the upper bands: the scalar construction is not a rule that all generated level words lie in a small positive signed range. The original SSE rounding environment, floating exception state, invalid conversion behavior outside ordinary finite input conditions, and imported random-number range/distribution are not established by this audit. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9E59–0x1C9E79`, `0x1F5268–0x1F5275`, `0x1F8B4B–0x1F8B58`, `0x1C9F15–0x1C9F1E`.]

## Band-zero fixed stock and retained members

At band 0 the local item begins with `+0x04/+0x08`, tier byte `+0x0C`, byte `+0x0E`, extension count and the entire extension area zero. Its level word is 1. The following insertions and changes occur in this order; omitted semantic members retain those values. Later rows reuse the same local item. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9BE6–0x1C9D8D`.]

| Ordered stock operation | Category | Subtype | Tier `+0x0C` | Material `+0x0D` | Extra random admission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First fixed insertion | 11 | 12 | 0 | 24 | None |
| Second fixed insertion | 24 | 0 | 0 | 1 | None |
| Optional insertions, checked in ascending tier order | 24 | 0 | 1, 2, 3, 4 | 1 | A separate draw for each tier; signed remainder modulo 10, 20, 30, 40 respectively must be zero |
| Next fixed insertion | 23 | 0 | 0 | 2 | None |
| Next fixed insertion | 23 | 1 | 0 | 2 | None |
| Final fixed insertion | 20 | Selected below | 0 | 0 | One draw, signed remainder modulo 6 |

For the last row, remainders 0 through 5 select subtypes `0x22`, `0x23`, `0x13`, `0x1A`, `0x1E`, `0x57` respectively. These checks do not retry. All four optional checks run, whether earlier ones succeeded or failed. The subsequent write of word `0x0200` at item `+0x0C` resets any tier left by an optional insertion before the first category-23 item. These are numeric selector identities; this audit does not assign display names to them. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9C85–0x1C9D8D`; `decompiled/client/functions/005c9b30.c`.]

## Per-band generated stock and class-selector admission

After any band-zero fixed items, one draw's signed remainder modulo 3 determines the number of generated category-2 items for that band. Under ordinary nonnegative random results it is 0, 1 or 2, processed sequentially. For each item, the tier starts with a fresh draw's signed remainder modulo 2. Three further draws are made unconditionally, in order: remainder modulo 100 equal to zero adds 1, then remainder modulo 1000 equal to zero adds 1, then remainder modulo 10000 equal to zero adds 1. A signed upper cap of 4 follows. A fifth draw then chooses the item selector: even remainder modulo 2 calls `0x1F51E0`; odd calls `0x1F8AD0`. Each receives an output-item address, the band result, tier and the 32-bit selector value -1. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9D92–0x1C9E79`.]

Before that family-selection draw, the stock body's local item has its semantic members reset to the empty defaults, including level word 1 and zero extension area. The chosen output is then assigned into it with `0x02C5E0`. Its original category byte is zero-extended into item `+0x08`, then its category byte alone is replaced with 2. Subtype, random `+0x04`, tier, material, level word, byte `+0x0E`, extension data and extension count retain the selected candidate's assigned values. The resulting category-2 item is inserted into descriptor inventory group 0. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9DEF–0x1C9EB3`; `decompiled/client/functions/0042c5e0.c`.]

The last argument of each item selector admits its numbered branches: a branch runs when that argument equals its listed selector value or is signed-negative. The actual stock call passes -1, so every numbered branch runs. These selectors do not read an actor class, specialization, descriptor numeric role, kind or descriptor level. Their branch numbers may be useful to other callers as class filters, but this route supplies the all-branches admission explicitly. No symbolic class names are established here. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9E59–0x1C9E79`, `0x1F5288–0x1F5509`, `0x1F8B79–0x1F8D05`.]

## Selector at RVA 0x1F51E0

This selector creates an initially empty local item-candidate collection. Every candidate has subtype 0, auxiliary `+0x08` zero, byte `+0x0E` zero, the supplied tier's low byte, the supplied level's low word, zero extension area and zero extension count. Each candidate gets a new draw's signed remainder modulo 100 in its 32-bit `+0x04` immediately before append. Under selector -1 its first 19 candidates occur in these exact groups and order. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1F520E–0x1F55CA`; `decompiled/client/functions/005f51e0.c`, with native local-collection verification.]

| Branch selector | Material byte | Category sequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 7, 4, 5, 6 |
| 3 | 25 | 7, 7, 4, 5, 6 |
| 2 | 26 | 7, 7, 4, 5, 6 |
| 4 | 27 | 7, 7, 4, 5, 6 |

Two more candidates always follow those branches: category 8, then category 9. For each, a fresh parity draw selects material 11 for even and 12 for odd, followed by a fresh modulo-100 draw for `+0x04`, then append. The duplicated category-7 entries are separate candidates with separate random fields; they are not collapsed. A final fresh draw is divided unsigned by the candidate count, 21 for this stock call. The remainder selects a zero-based candidate in append order. Its semantic fields are copied into the caller-supplied output item, and the temporary candidate allocation is released. Thus this call makes 24 random draws: 19 candidate-field draws, two material draws, two further candidate-field draws and one final selection draw. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1F55D6–0x1F5698`; item append `0x0C7830–0x0C78B1`; item copy `0x00EE70–0x00EF10`; `decompiled/client/functions/004c7830.c`.]

## Selector at RVA 0x1F8AD0

This selector also uses an initially empty local candidate collection. Every candidate has category 3, auxiliary `+0x08` zero, byte `+0x0E` zero, the supplied tier's low byte, the supplied level's low word, zero extension area and zero extension count. Each append gets a fresh modulo-100 draw in `+0x04`. Under selector -1 the following eleven candidates are appended in order. The material and subtype changes are explicit; a row that changes only subtype retains the current material. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1F8AFE–0x1F8D96`; `decompiled/client/functions/005f8ad0.c`.]

| Branch selector | Ordered subtypes | Material byte and additional draws |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Remainder modulo 3; then 15 plus another remainder modulo 3; then 13 | Material 1. The subtype draw precedes the field draw for each of the first two candidates. |
| 4 | 3, 5, 4 | Retains material 1. |
| 2 | 6, 8 | Material 2. After appending subtype 6, one random draw is discarded before assigning subtype 8 and drawing its field value. |
| 3 | 10, 11, 12 | The first two retain material 2. For subtype 12, a fresh parity draw chooses material 12 for even and 11 for odd, before its field draw. |

A final fresh draw is divided unsigned by the candidate count, 11 for this stock call. The remainder selects a zero-based candidate in append order; its semantic fields are copied into the supplied output and the temporary candidate allocation is released. The complete selector call makes 16 random draws: eleven candidate-field draws, two subtype draws, the discarded draw, one material draw and one final selection draw. The discarded draw is part of the original ordering. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1F8B86–0x1F8DE4`; `decompiled/client/functions/004c7830.c`, RVA `0x0C7830`; `decompiled/client/functions/0040ee70.c`, RVA `0x00EE70`.]

## Final item per band, quantity and insertion order

After all generated category-2 items for a band, one new parity draw is always made. An even result adds nothing; an odd result constructs category 1, subtype 7, using the band's low-word level. Its `+0x04/+0x08`, tier, material, byte `+0x0E`, extension area and extension count are reset to zero. It is inserted into group 0 before advancing to the next band. No descriptor-level or role test is added at this point. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9EC7–0x1C9F52`.]

Every insertion in this stock route initially contributes quantity 1. Inventory-add `0x06EBE0` works on a semantic item copy, defaults the contribution to 1, and for the reached categories 11/subtype 12, 24, 23 and 20 instead obtains the signed level word and normalizes the copy's level to 1. Those supplied level words are already 1. Categories 2 and 1 keep contribution 1 without that normalization, so their possibly truncated item-level words do not become quantities. None of the produced categories enters inventory-add's category-12 or category-13 counter routes. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x06EC4A–0x06ED12`; `decompiled/client/functions/0046ebe0.c`.]

Inventory-add grows the outer group collection when needed; group 0 is initially an empty entry collection, with 12-byte group descriptors and `0x11C`-byte entries. Each entry contains a 32-bit quantity followed by its semantic item. Scanning uses ascending entry order. It remembers the first entry whose quantity is exactly zero, while continuing the equality search. The stackable-category test is applied to the supplied item and admits categories 1, 10, 12, 13, 11 and 21. In this route that enables stacking only for category 1 and the fixed category-11 item. An eligible first equal item receives a 32-bit addition of the contribution and returns immediately; existing quantity positivity is not required by that comparison path. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x06EC26–0x06EC45`, `0x06ED12–0x06EE1E`, `0x07F9F0–0x07FA0F`; `decompiled/client/functions/00487380.c`, `00427a80.c`, `0047f9f0.c`.]

For items produced here, the extension count is zero. Equality `0x02F4A0` therefore compares category, subtype, 32-bit `+0x04`, byte `+0x0E`, 32-bit `+0x08`, tier, raw level word, material and extension count, and performs no extension-entry comparisons. Padding is not compared. If there is no eligible equality match, the remembered first zero-quantity entry receives the semantic item and quantity 1; otherwise an entry is appended in insertion order with quantity then semantic item copy. Category-2, category-24, category-23 and category-20 stock does not merge merely because its fields repeat. On the freshly constructed descriptor there are no old zero slots or negative quantities; later repeated category-1 items can merge while retaining the first occurrence's position. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x02F4A0–0x02F54E`, `0x06EE21–0x06EE95`, `0x086700–0x086773`; `decompiled/client/functions/0042f4a0.c`, `00486700.c`.]

The stock helper performs no inventory-wide clear or replacement. Its observed caller creates a fresh descriptor first, and the helper then inserts into it. Calling this helper on an existing inventory would preserve earlier entries except for the insertion service's matching additions or zero-slot reuse; it would not itself refresh the stock by replacing the group. The later full-inventory assignment into an actor is a distinct operation: grouped contents are assigned first, followed by inventory scalar `+0x0C`, embedded semantic item `+0x10`, then counters `+0x128/+0x12C`. No aliasing of the descriptor's stock-item addresses into the actor is established by that assignment. The lower allocation/copy helpers and actor lifetime are not comprehensively audited here. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, RVAs `0x1C9B30–0x1C9F68`, `0x1BCDA7–0x1BCE53`, `0x2103A6–0x2103B3`, `0x04AFF0–0x04B02F`; `decompiled/client/functions/0044ad30.c`, `0044aff0.c`.]

## Random boundaries and remaining limits

The random calls above use the named import `MSVCR110.dll!rand`, whose original IAT slot is RVA `0x2FC468`. No seed assignment, reseeding, dedicated per-descriptor generator or random-state save/restore appears in the stock body or either closed item selector. Each ordinary band consumes, in order, its level draw, its generated-item-count draw, the complete sequential draws for each admitted generated item, and the final category-1 parity draw. Band 0 inserts its four optional-stock draws and its subtype-choice draw between the level and generated-item-count draws. Each generated item consumes its four tier draws, family draw, then all 24 or 16 selector draws before insertion. This gives three direct band draws, five additional direct draws at band 0, and 29 or 21 total draws per generated category-2 item. The two upstream descriptor draws are separate and precede this stock call. These are call counts and ordering, not claims of uniform outcome probabilities. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, import directory and IAT at RVA `0x2FC468`; RVAs `0x1BCDE4–0x1BCE1E`, `0x1C9B30–0x1C9F68`, `0x1F51E0–0x1F56B5`, `0x1F8AD0–0x1F8E01`.]

The closed item selectors append semantic copies to local collections and choose among those copies; no further item-stat, material-generation or power helper is called on this route. Container allocation, reallocation, disposal, memory validity, exception unwinding and imported runtime internals remain supplied services. Ordinary conclusions assume their successful completion and valid source collections. No locking, transaction, rollback or atomic publication guarantee is established by these inspected instructions. The wider producers of the pointed-object discriminator and position collections, the two unused scalar arguments' sources, actor creation admission, stock persistence/repopulation schedule, vendor UI export, economic value, display names and other descriptor/item-generation branches remain unresolved. No server equivalence is inferred. [Evidence: `binary/Cube.exe`, the bounded bodies and upstream intervals cited above; `decompiled/client/functions/004c6940.c`, `004c6840.c`, `004c7830.c`, `0046ebe0.c`, `005c9b30.c`, `005f51e0.c`, `005f8ad0.c`.]
